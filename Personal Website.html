01 — About

A bit about me, and the problems I like to work on.

I'm a software engineer focused on machine learning. I like problems that sit at the boundary between research and production — where a clever idea has to survive contact with real data, real latency budgets, and real users.

Over the last few years I've worked on large language models, retrieval systems, and the training and serving infrastructure that holds it all together. I care about reproducibility, honest evaluation, and code that other people can actually read six months later.

Outside of work I read papers slowly, contribute to a couple of open-source projects, and try to build small useful things on the weekend.
